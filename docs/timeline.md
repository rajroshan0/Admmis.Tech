# Timeline — 8 weeks to 1 January 2027

The original estimate was ~14 weeks. To hit 1 Jan we run design, build and content **in parallel** and reduce launch scope. Anything not in "Launch scope" goes to Phase 2.

## Launch scope (v1, 1 Jan)
- Home
- Services hub + **8–10 service pages** (Development, Mobile apps, Websites, AI products & agents, QA & testing, Audit, Outstaffing, Digital marketing, Offline marketing)
- Our work: **4–6 case studies** from current clients (with their permission)
- Pricing (packages in NPR + USD, quote form)
- **"On rent" page:** website / AI agent / team on rent, monthly plans, what's included, FAQ (code ownership, minimum term, support hours)
- About, Careers (we are hiring), Contact
- Blog with 3 launch articles
- Opening event page
- Legal: privacy, terms, cookies
- Lead pipeline, analytics, CMS for editors

## Phase 2 (from Jan 2027)
Nepali language (~Feb) · cost calculator · AI chat assistant · industry landing pages (retail, restaurants, pharmacy, education…) · leads into ADMIS CRM · events/webinars section · client portal
