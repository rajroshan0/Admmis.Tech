## 2. Proposal: monthly rental per size band (NPR)

| Service on rent | S (1–10 staff) | M (11–50) | L (51–250) | XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Website on rent (site, hosting, updates, support) | 25,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 | quote |

USD = NPR ÷ 150, plus about 30% for international clients.

**What the market charges:** a basic one-off website costs NPR 15,000–50,000. Social-media management costs NPR 20,000–75,000/month. An SEO retainer costs NPR 40,000–100,000/month. Our S-band rental costs more than a cheap one-off site, but it includes hosting, updates and support. The page will show a 12-month cost comparison.

⚠ **Check:** a senior at NPR 180,000 is about USD 1,200 (USD 1,560 with the international uplift). Directory median rates are about USD 37/h, roughly USD 5,900/month. For international clients we may be **underpriced by 3–4×**. Option: a separate USD rate card for outstaffing, e.g. senior USD 2,500–3,500/month.

## 4. Rental terms (proposal)
Setup fee: 1× the monthly price (website and AI agent), none for team. Minimum term: 6 months, with 10% off for 12 months. Support: S 4 h, M 8 h, L 16 h per month. Critical response: within 4 working hours (1 h for L). Payment monthly in advance (bank, eSewa, Khalti; USD by bank or Wise). Content and domain always belong to the client. Code buyout costs 6× the monthly price.
