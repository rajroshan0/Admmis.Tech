## intro
- intro: An audit is an honest review by someone outside your project. We look at your website or app and tell you what is good, what is weak and what to fix first. An audit helps when your site is slow, your code is old, your visits are falling, or you plan a redesign or a launch. You get findings in plain words, not a list of technical terms.
