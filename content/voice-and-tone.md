# ADMMIS.TECH voice and tone

## Voice in one line
We sound like a skilled neighbour who builds software: professional, warm and direct. We are in Nepal and we work to international standards. We never boast. We show.

## Four rules
1. **Plain English.** Many readers use English as a second language. Average sentence 20 words or fewer. One idea per sentence.
2. **You before we.** Talk about the reader's business first. Use "you" more than "we".
3. **Active voice.** Write "We build your site", not "Your site will be built".
4. **Proof, not praise.** Say what we do and what it gives the reader. No claim we cannot prove.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Get a website that brings you customers." | "Leverage cutting-edge digital synergies." |
| "We reply on WhatsApp the same working day." (only once Roshan confirms) | "Lightning-fast support." |
| "Pay monthly, or pay once for a project." | "Best prices in Nepal." |
| "Tell us what you sell. We suggest a plan." | "World-class, award-winning team." (no awards unless proven) |
| Explain a term once: "an AI agent is software that answers customers for you." | Use jargon with no explanation. |

## Truth rules
- No invented clients, numbers, results, awards, years, team size or testimonials.
- Unknown fact: write `TODO(roshan): <question>`.
- Never type a price. Use `{{price:<id>}}` from the CMS.

## Word list

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| ADMMIS.TECH (capitals, one word, dot) | Admmis Tech, ADMMIS Tech |
| website, app, AI agent | solution, platform (unless a real platform), "cutting-edge" |
| on rent (our product name for monthly subscription) | "SaaS" without explanation |
| get a quote, talk to us | "synergy", "leverage", "best-in-class", "world-class" |
| team, developers, designers | "resources", "headcount" |
| NPR 1,000 and USD 10 (code first, comma thousands; format only) | Rs., /-, "50k" |
| Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Pokhara, Janakpurdham, Nepal | Nepali spellings that vary (use these exact ones) |
| small business, shop, company | "SME" (explain once if used) |

## CTA wording

| Use | Label | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Get a quote** | header, hero, final CTA, every page end |
| Secondary | **See our services** / **See plans on rent** | hero, sections |
| WhatsApp | **Chat on WhatsApp** | sticky bar, contact |
| Call | **Call us** | sticky bar, contact |
| Form submit | **Send my request** | forms |

One primary CTA per screen. A CTA says what happens next.

## Translatable for Nepali (from February 2027)
- No idioms, slang, jokes or wordplay ("kick-start", "hit the ground running", "a no-brainer").
- Each text block stands alone. Do not rely on the sentence before or after.
- Avoid sentences that depend on English word order, such as stacked nouns ("customer service automation workflow").
- Keep numbers, names and prices outside sentences where we can, so they can be placeholders.
- Leave room: Nepali text is often 20 to 30 percent longer.
- Keep brand and product terms in Latin script: ADMMIS.TECH, WhatsApp, AI.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone |
|---|---|
| Hero, services | Confident and clear |
| Pricing, rent | Honest and exact; say what is not included |
| Forms, errors | Calm and helpful; say how to fix it |
| Success messages | Warm, short, say what happens next |
| Legal | Plain, but reviewed by a lawyer or Roshan |
