7. Do and don't

Do

  • Use vermilion for the one primary CTA per screen
  • Keep marigold for decoration and highlights, never for text on light
  • Use arches and circles as hero frames
  • Keep photography warm and natural

Don't

  • Do not put text in marigold on a light background (1.77:1)
  • Do not use more than one vermilion block per viewport
  • Do not use thin geometric fonts below 16 px
  • Do not use temple or religious icons as decoration

8. Why it fits the audience

Reads as local and warm at first glance, which builds trust with Nepali SMEs and shop owners. The bright palette still looks credible to international buyers because layout, spacing and type stay clean and disciplined. Lowest risk of feeling cold or generic. Easiest to re-skin: change two variables for a rental client.

Risks. Strong red-orange can read as "retail / food brand" if overused. Needs discipline on accent use. Overlaps visually with many South Asian consumer brands.

Performance. Hero image is the only large asset; fonts: 700 (display) / 600 (H2-H3) headings + 400 / 600 body; no decorative images; reduced motion respected. Multi-brand (ADR-013): this direction = the token table above; a rental client changes only the colour tokens, radius and font variables.
