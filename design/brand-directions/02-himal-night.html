7. Do and don't

Do

  • Alternate dark hero with one light section (#F3F6FB) so long pages breathe
  • Keep the teal for the CTA and active states only
  • Use the gold for one highlight per screen
  • Keep glow effects as static gradients (no blur animations)

Don't

  • Do not use teal text on the light section (use ink)
  • Do not animate more than the hero chips, and respect reduced motion
  • Do not use pure black; stay on the navy
  • Do not stack more than two glass layers (slow on low-end Android)

8. Why it fits the audience

Strongest "technology and AI" signal, the right look for B2B, rental plans and international clients who judge by polish. Dark-first suits the product tools and cost calculator later. Using the model as a warm focus in a cool scene stops it feeling like a generic SaaS site.

Risks. Dark themes are less common among Nepali SMEs and may feel less approachable to small shop owners. Needs extra care with contrast in light sections and in forms. Gradient glows can cost performance if not kept static.

Performance. Hero image is the only large asset; fonts: 700 headings + 400 body; no decorative images; reduced motion respected. Multi-brand (ADR-013): this direction = the token table above; a rental client changes only the colour tokens, radius and font variables.
