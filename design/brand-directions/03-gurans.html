7. Do and don't

Do

  • Use serif only for headings, sans for everything else
  • Use pink for accents and links, green for the primary CTA
  • Number sections and lists (01, 02, 03) for rhythm
  • Leave generous white space; let type carry the layout

Don't

  • Do not set body copy in the serif
  • Do not use pink for large background fields (reads as a fashion brand)
  • Do not use more than 3 type sizes in one section
  • Do not crop the model's face; crop at the waist

8. Why it fits the audience

Feels established and premium, so it supports higher-priced packages, rentals and large-project quotes. The Nepali flower story is memorable and local without being decorative. The serif headline gives Devanagari strong readability and a distinct editorial voice no other tech agency in the region is likely to have.

Risks. Most conservative of the three; may feel less "tech" and more "design agency". Four font families add weight; we must subset and limit to the weights shown to keep the JS/LCP budget.

Performance. Hero image is the only large asset; fonts: 600 headings + 400 / 600 body; no decorative images; reduced motion respected. Multi-brand (ADR-013): this direction = the token table above; a rental client changes only the colour tokens, radius and font variables.
