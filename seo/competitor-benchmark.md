## 0. Method and confidence

- Method: web search on 2026-11-06 for Nepali web, software and marketing agencies; recorded what the results showed. I could not open each agency's full site in this pass, so many rows rely on directory listings and search snippets.

- Items I could not confirm are marked UNVERIFIED. A deeper manual pass on the first six agencies is recommended before Gate 1 pricing is final (Roshan or a team member opening each site; I can provide a checklist).
- Rates are as published; USD to NPR is not converted here (NPR/USD varies; pricing-model.md uses roughly NPR 150 per USD ÷ margin logic).
- FITTIN is a structural reference only (docs/reference-fittin.md).

Observations:
1. The market **competes on low entry price** (NPR 15,000-ish websites, under $25/h). We should not race to the bottom.
2. **Pricing is rarely public on agency own pages**; the information sits in blog guides. A clear price page is itself a differentiator.
3. **No Nepali agency in these results sells a monthly website rental, AI agent rental, or team rental with a public plan** (UNVERIFIED: only searched on 2026-11-06 with the tool available; a manual search of "website on rent Nepal" should confirm before we claim "first").
4. **Few combine development, AI and marketing** in one agency; Yeti Studio and Thulo touch two.
5. Pricing-model.md anchors vs market:
   - Website rental S band NPR 25,000/month is above a one-off basic site (NPR 15,000–40,000) but includes hosting, support and updates. The page must show a 12-month total-cost comparison.
   - Team rental: NPR 180,000 per senior per month is about $1,200 at NPR 150/USD (about $1,560 with the +30% international uplift). A full month at the GoodFirms median of $37/h x 160 h is about $5,900. Our monthly rate would be well below hourly-billed market medians. Either the plan is a deliberate low-price play or the price needs review. See Needs Roshan.
